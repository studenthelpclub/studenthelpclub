BPYG-171 Applied Ethics: opinion ko reasoned answer banayein

BPYG-171 ka course title Applied Ethics hai. Assignment mein ethical issue par strong personal opinion hona aur us opinion ko academic reasoning ke saath explain kar paana alag skills hain. Aapko sirf right ya wrong announce nahi karna. Question kis problem ko examine karta hai, kaunse principles relevant hain, aur different positions kya reasons deti hain, unhe course material ke context mein samajhna hai. Final answer ka focus argument par rahe, personal judgement ya social media debate par nahi.

Reading notebook mein issue, position, supporting reason aur objection ke four columns useful hain. Agar kisi case par do views milen toh pehle dono ko fair language mein record karein. Apne preferred view ko detailed aur opposite view ko weak one-line version dena comparison ko biased bana sakta hai. Assignment writing mein disagree kar sakte hain, lekin pehle jis position se disagree kar rahe hain uska accurate meaning samajhna zaroori hai.

Case ya issue padhte waqt facts aur assumptions alag rakhein

Question mein case diya ho toh supplied facts ko carefully note karein. Missing fact ko apni convenience se add karke preferred conclusion prove mat karein. Agar assumption discussion ke liye necessary hai toh use explicitly assumption batayein aur explain karein ki assumption badalne par reasoning kaise change ho sakti hai. Real-life example choose karte samay identifying private details remove karein. Kisi person ke intent ya wrongdoing ka claim without evidence assignment mein add na karein.

Hypothetical example use karna practice ka safe tareeka ho sakta hai, jab aap usko clearly hypothetical label karte hain. Lekin realistic sounding story ko actual incident mat present karein. Case notes mein stakeholders kaun hain aur kis decision ka analysis kar rahe hain, woh clarify karein. Stakeholder list ko bas names ki list na banayein; question ke relevant interests ya responsibilities note karein. Har ethical issue same decision framework demand nahi karta, isliye course context pehle dekhein.

Answer mein claim, reason aur objection ka connection

Ek main claim likhne ke baad poochhein: is claim ke support mein mere paas kya course-based reason hai? Reason ke baad poochhein: iska case ya question se connection kya hai? Phir relevant alternative position ya objection dekhein. Aapka answer long tab useful hota hai jab analysis aage badhta hai. Same moral judgement ko different words mein repeat karna analysis nahi. Conclusion body ke reasoning se nikle, opening mein decided result ko har paragraph se force na karwayein.

Question compare maangta ho toh common headings use karein. Explain maangta ho toh conceptual clarity priority hai. Critically discuss maangta ho toh description ke saath relevant objections aur limits address karein. Exact instruction ke according scope adjust karein. Yeh general writing advice hai; official word limit, choice pattern aur submission medium booklet se check karna zaroori hai. Guide ka paragraph structure academic instruction ka replacement nahi hai.

Outline example: professional dilemma par planning exercise

Practice ke liye ek hypothetical professional decision imagine karein jahan two responsibilities conflict kar rahi hon. Yeh current BPYG-171 paper ka question ya ready answer nahi. Outline ke first part mein conflict ki boundary aur known facts likhein. Second part mein relevant course concepts ke placeholders rakhein. Third part mein possible position A ke reasons aur limits, aur fourth mein position B ke reasons aur limits note karein. Har reason ke saath unit reference ka box rakhein.

Agla part compare kare ki dono positions kis interest ya responsibility ko priority deti hain, lekin actual points aap course reading ke baad fill karein. Last part mein carefully limited conclusion aur remaining uncertainty note karein. Agar additional fact ke bina judgement nahi ho sakta, uncertainty ko honestly explain karna better hai. Actual assignment mein hypothetical case add karna tabhi useful hoga jab question ke focus se match kare. Kisi outline ko submit-ready text mat samjhein.

Own-word writing: labels nahi, reasoning explain karein

Passage padhne ke baad apne notes se ek friend ko concept explain karne ka practice karein. Agar sirf label ya definition repeat kar pa rahe hain, toh reasoning par wapas reading karein. Book band karke answer draft karein, phir book ke meaning se compare karein. Technical term same reh sakta hai, lekin sentence aur example aapki understanding se develop hon. Direct quotation ko quote mark aur source ke saath identify karein; fake quotation se answer ko authority mat dein.

Own-word writing mein tone bhi matter karti hai. Kisi position ko stupid, selfish ya obvious kehkar dismiss karne se pehle uska argument examine karein. Neutral language reader ko comparison samajhne mein help karti hai. Aap firm conclusion likh sakte hain, lekin conclusion ki boundaries clear rakhein. Ek limited case par decision se har possible situation ke liye universal rule announce karna avoid karein, jab tak question aur course reasoning us wider claim ko support na karein.

Common mistakes: moral slogan, law aur ethics ka mix-up

Pehli common problem hai slogan ko analysis bana dena. Fix: slogan ko side rakhkar precise claim aur reason likhein. Dusri problem hai legally allowed ko automatically ethically justified keh dena, ya ethical concern ko automatically legal violation batana. Agar question law aur ethics ka relation discuss karta hai toh relevant unit padhein aur distinction carefully handle karein. Current law quote kar rahe hain toh official text aur applicable context verify karein.

Teesri problem hai opposite position ko straw version bana dena. Fix: course text mein us position ke strongest stated reasons check karein. Chauthi problem hai imagined details ko case facts mein mix karna. Fix: facts aur assumptions ki two-column list banayein. Final draft mein har paragraph question se connected ho, relevant objection ka fair treatment ho, aur conclusion reasoning se follow kare. Private reference PDF ko optional learning support samjhein, final authority nahi.

FAQ: Kya assignment mein apni opinion likh sakte hain?

Jahan question analysis ya evaluation maangta hai, apni reasoned assessment useful ho sakti hai. Lekin personal preference ko evidence ka substitute mat banayein. Course concepts, relevant reasons aur limits explain karein. Sirf mujhe lagta hai likhne se analysis complete nahi hota; reader ko samajhna chahiye ki aap us position tak kaise pahunche.

FAQ: Kya real incident ka example dena zaroori hai?

Nahi, jab tak question ya official instruction demand na kare. Carefully labelled hypothetical example bhi concept ko samjhane mein help kar sakta hai. Real example ho toh facts verify karein aur private details avoid karein. Example ko answer ka centre tabhi banayein jab woh actual question ki demand ko serve karta ho.
