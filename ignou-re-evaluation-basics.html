IGNOU re-evaluation and answer-script copy: how to decide

What the official IGNOU re-evaluation portal says, how a copy of the answer script differs, which assessments are excluded, and a checklist before you apply.

Separate a low mark from a record problem

When a result is lower than you expected, it is tempting to apply for re-evaluation at once. First work out what the issue really is. Are the marks low, is a result missing, is an assignment entry not showing, or does a course code look wrong? These are different problems, and they do not all use the same process. Re-evaluation is a specific academic service. It is not a general fix for every record problem.

Save your current result and write your concern in one line. That makes the purpose of your request clear and lowers the chance of filling in the wrong form. This guide helps you organise the decision. It does not state a current fee, last date or processing time, and it does not promise that marks will go up.

What the official portal says

The IGNOU Online Reevaluation portal, when we read it on 1 October 2026, said the following in its general instructions:

  • You can apply for re-evaluation and for a copy of the answer script once the Term End Examination (TEE) result is declared and uploaded on the IGNOU website.
  • Re-evaluation is allowed in the TEE only. It is not available for projects or dissertations, practicals and lab courses, workshops, assignments or seminars.
  • Re-evaluation and answer-script copies are not available for certain multiple-choice (OMR) exams. The portal lists the course codes. Check the live list for your course.
  • The copy can be sent as a scan to the email address you give in the form, or as a photocopy by post.
  • Fees can be paid by debit or credit card or by net banking, through a payment link generated after you save the form.

These points are context, not your eligibility ruling. Match your exam cycle, course code and assessment type with the instructions, and download and read the full instruction document on the portal. Seeing a mark on the result page does not make every request eligible.

A missing result is not zero marks

If a result does not show, check the course and exam details in your official record. Read what "pending", "incomplete" or "not available" means in the current guidance. Do not read a blank field as zero. If your TEE result is available and you want a review of the evaluation, re-evaluation may be relevant, subject to eligibility. Turning a record mismatch into a marks-review request can create the wrong follow-up and extra cost.

In a private note, write down the programme, course, exam cycle, displayed result, the date you checked and the issue. Do not post your enrolment or ID details in public groups. Ask official support a precise question, for example: "This course result shows as unavailable. What is the correct status-check or enquiry route?" If the answer is unclear, ask for a written instruction or a link.

Answer-script copy and re-evaluation are different services

A copy lets you see your written answers. Re-evaluation asks the university to evaluate the answers again. Getting a copy does not start a marks review. Decide what you want: to see your answers, to have the evaluation reviewed, or to enquire about a record. Do not choose an option only because you are upset about the mark.

The portal also describes how the two relate. If you applied for re-evaluation and then applied for a copy, the copy is provided only if the script has not already been processed for re-evaluation. If it has been processed, the copy cannot be provided. The same instructions say you can apply for a copy before re-evaluation, or apply for both together in one go. If you want both, read the current instructions on order before you pay for either.

Two illustrations (not real rulings)

First case. A student's assignment entry is missing, but the theory TEE result is available. The student wants a review of the theory marks and also wants the assignment entry fixed. These are two concerns and belong on two lists. Theory review depends on eligibility and re-evaluation conditions. The assignment entry needs the correct enquiry route. One form will not solve both.

Second case. A student only wants to read the answer script to learn from it. The immediate purpose may be a copy, but the eligibility and timing conditions still need to be checked at the official source. Writing "what I actually want" in your decision note helps. Ignore private claims that marks will definitely increase.

Checklist before you apply

  • The assessment is eligible and is a TEE.
  • The result is available and the course code is correct.
  • You know the current deadline, fee and required documents from the official instructions, not from an old tutorial.
  • You understand the payment and refund conditions.
  • You know which service you want: copy, re-evaluation or both, and in what order.
  • You accept the cost and the risk. Nobody can promise that a pass or higher marks will follow.

Remembering your own performance is not a substitute for the real answer script. Do not assume other academic steps pause while a request is pending. If guidance conflicts, ask official support, and keep verified and unanswered points separate in your notes.

Fill in the form and review the payment

Use the official portal or a link from the main IGNOU website. Fill in the required fields, then read the summary: course, exam details, chosen service and the spelling of your email address. Upload only the documents the form asks for. Never send a password or OTP to a helper.

Read the total shown and the current payment instructions. Save the receipt, reference and submission status. A bank debit does not by itself confirm that the application was accepted. If payment shows pending or the page errors, check the official guidance before paying again. When you contact support, give references and details only, never card numbers or credentials.

Follow up and check the final record

Use the status process and contact route on the current portal. Do not rely on group stories for processing time. If you raise a query, give the exact issue and your reference, and do not send documents that are not needed. Check the final result in the official record, and do not build your next academic step on an unofficial message. Save a screenshot and the date you checked.

Re-evaluation depends on university process. The aim of this guide is to help you prepare an informed request, not to promise a result. Keep your study planning separate from the administrative follow-up, so you stay organised while a request is pending.

Is this the form for assignment marks?

No. The portal's general instructions place assignments outside the TEE re-evaluation scope. For an assignment record or marks concern, find the correct enquiry route through official support. Do not apply for the wrong service.

Does asking for a copy mean my marks are reviewed automatically?

No. A copy is access to your answer script. Re-evaluation is a review request. If you want both, check the order and timing rules in the current instructions instead of guessing.

Will re-evaluation increase my marks?

Nobody can promise that. Decide using the current rules, the eligible assessment and the university process. Treat private "guaranteed pass" claims as no evidence. Verify the final result in the official record.

Official sources

The official IGNOU Online Reevaluation portal was read on 1 October 2026. Fees, dates and the list of excluded courses can change, so read the instructions again when you apply.
