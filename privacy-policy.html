Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

At Student Help Club (accessible from https://studenthelpclub.in/), one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors and students. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by us and how we securely use it.

1. Information Collection and Use

We collect personal information such as your name, WhatsApp number, email address, and subject codes only when you voluntarily initiate a purchase or request support on our platform. This data is strictly utilized to facilitate the fast delivery of your academic reference materials and to provide exceptional customer assistance. We use Firebase (a Google service) to securely store our database information.

2. Log Files

Student Help Club follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable. The purpose of the information is for analyzing trends, administering the site, and tracking users' movement on the website.

3. Cookies and Google AdSense

Like any other website, our portal uses 'cookies'. These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. Furthermore, our website uses Google AdSense to display relevant advertisements. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses DART cookies to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our site and other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting your Google Ad Settings.

4. Comments and Public Discussions

When you post a comment on our Important Updates blog articles, the name and text you provide are stored in our secure database to foster a community discussion. These comments are public; therefore, we strongly advise you not to share sensitive personal details (like passwords or full enrollment numbers) in the comment section.

5. Data Security & Third Parties

We implement industry-standard security measures to ensure your data remains safe. We never sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to others. We may share generic aggregated demographic information not linked to any personal identification information regarding visitors and users with our business partners and trusted affiliates for the purposes outlined above.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact our support team.

Reach out via email: officialstudenthelpclub@gmail.com

Or visit our Contact Page
