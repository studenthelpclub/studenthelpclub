Topics covered in this PDF

BECE-214 (Agricultural Development in India) - the questions solved in this PDF are on these topics:

  • Agricultural diversification and agricultural policy
  • Forests, water resources, agricultural productivity
  • Green Revolution and local self-government in rural development

These topics are taken from the questions in this PDF. Check the official syllabus and assignment paper for your session with IGNOU.

Assignment pattern

As printed on the first page of the PDF:

  • The cover prints: answer all questions, total marks 100
  • Section I: 2 long answers, about 500 words, 2 x 20 = 40 marks
  • Section II: 4 medium answers, about 250 words, 4 x 12 = 48 marks
  • Section III: short notes, about 100 words, 2 x 6 = 12 marks

Always check your session's assignment paper and submission deadline on the official IGNOU website or with your study centre.
