Topics covered in this PDF

BECC-131 (Principles of Microeconomics I) - the questions solved in this PDF are on these topics:

  • Determinants of demand, cross-price elasticity, isoquants and producer equilibrium
  • Production possibility curve, long-run average cost, stages of production, scale economies
  • Scarcity, supply and demand equilibrium, sunk and fixed cost, cardinal and ordinal utility
