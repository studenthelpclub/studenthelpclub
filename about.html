About Student Help Club

An independent student-run service from Delhi, offering study and reference PDFs for IGNOU courses.

Who We Are

Student Help Club (SHC) is run by Vishal Kumar from Delhi. It is an independent student-run service offering solved-question study and reference PDFs for IGNOU courses.

Student Help Club is not affiliated with, endorsed by or an official portal of Indira Gandhi National Open University (IGNOU). For official course requirements, notices and submission rules, check IGNOU directly.

Our PDFs are for learning and reference, not for copying or submitting as your own assessed work. We do not guarantee marks, acceptance or error-free answers. Check the material against your course books and current official instructions.

Visit the official IGNOU website

What We Offer

Solved-question PDFs for study and reference. Availability may vary by course code, academic session, medium and format. Confirm the details before ordering.

How Ordering Works

Send your course code, session, medium and required format on WhatsApp. Ask about availability, price, a sample and delivery terms before paying.
