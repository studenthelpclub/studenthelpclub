Topics covered in this PDF

BEGC-112 (British Literature: Early Twentieth Century) - the questions solved in this PDF are on these topics:

  • Explanation of four verse passages, characteristics of modernism, Sons and Lovers, Mrs. Dalloway
  • The poetry of Stephen Spender, World War 1 in British writing, Yeats and The Second Coming, stream of consciousness

Assignment pattern

As printed on the first page of the PDF:

  • The cover prints: answer all questions, maximum marks 100
  • Section A: explain four passages with reference to the context, 10 x 4 = 40
  • Section B: four short notes, 4 x 5 = 20
  • Section B part III short essays and Section C: marks not shown on the cover preview

Always check your session's assignment paper and submission deadline on the official IGNOU website or with your study centre.
