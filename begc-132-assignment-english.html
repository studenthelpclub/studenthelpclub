Topics covered in this PDF

BEGC-132 (Selections from Indian Writing: Cultural Diversity) - the questions solved in this PDF are on these topics:

  • Explanation of verse passages with reference to the context, change of perspective in Sapa Vimochanam, oral tradition in North East Indian literature
