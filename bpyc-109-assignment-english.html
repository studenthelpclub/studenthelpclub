Assignment pattern

As printed on the first page of the PDF:

  • The cover prints: answer all five questions, equal marks, Questions 1 and 2 in about 400 words each, attempt all parts of any question with parts
  • Questions 1 and 2 each print an OR alternative; the PDF solves the first option of each only
  • Question 3: any two of four, about 200 words, 2 x 10 = 20. Question 4: any four of six, about 150 words, 4 x 5 = 20
  • Question 5: short notes on any five of eight, about 100 words each

Always check your session's assignment paper and submission deadline on the official IGNOU website or with your study centre.
