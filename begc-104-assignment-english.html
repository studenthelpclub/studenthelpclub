Topics covered in this PDF

BEGC-104 (British Poetry and Drama: 14th to 17th Century) - the questions solved in this PDF are on these topics:

  • Metaphysical poetry, the Spenserian stanza, explanation of three passages with reference to the context
  • The Sun Rising, social criticism in The Wife of Bath's Prologue, themes of Shakespeare's Sonnets

These topics are taken from the questions in this PDF. Check the official syllabus and assignment paper for your session with IGNOU.

Assignment pattern

As printed on the first page of the PDF:

  • The cover prints: answer all questions, maximum marks 100
  • Section A: two short notes of about 200 words, 5 x 2 = 10
  • Section B: three passages with reference to the context, about 300 words each, 3 x 10 = 30
  • Section C: three questions of about 800 words each, 3 x 20 = 60

Always check your session's assignment paper and submission deadline on the official IGNOU website or with your study centre.
