Topics covered in this PDF

BPYM-162 (Ethics: An Introduction) - the questions solved in this PDF are on these topics:

  • Kant's deontological ethics, approaches to the study of ethics
  • Aristotle's virtue ethics, the four Purusarthas, moral norm, natural moral law
  • Consequentialism, Plato's cardinal virtues, Karmavada, moral relativism, short notes

These topics are taken from the questions in this PDF. Check the official syllabus and assignment paper for your session with IGNOU.

Assignment pattern

As printed on the first page of the PDF:

  • The cover prints: answer all five questions, equal marks (100 in total)
  • Questions 1 and 2 in about 400 words each, 20 marks each, with an Or option
  • Question 3: any two of four, about 200 words each. Question 4: about 150 words each, parts a to f. Question 5: any five of eight, about 100 words each
  • If any question has more than one part, the cover says to attempt all parts

Always check your session's assignment paper and submission deadline on the official IGNOU website or with your study centre.
