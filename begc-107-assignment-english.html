Topics covered in this PDF

BEGC-107 (British Poetry and Drama - 17th and 18th Century) - the questions solved in this PDF are on these topics:

  • Marlowian tragedy, John Webster's satire, the Augustan Age, pastoral poetry, the Cardinal in The Duchess of Malfi
  • Power and corruption in The Duchess of Malfi, Milton's L'Allegro and Il Penseroso, Pope's An Essay on Criticism, Mac Flecknoe, Jacobean drama, The Rape of the Lock, Lycidas
