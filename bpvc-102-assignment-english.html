Topics covered in this PDF

BPVC-102 (Food Chemistry and Nutrition) - the questions solved in this PDF are on these topics:

  • Food chemistry, vitamins and minerals, good nutrition and balanced diets
  • Nutritive value of agricultural produce, food additives
  • Part B activities: heating sugar, preservatives, boiling eggs, rancidity of oils, vitamin A collage, curd

These topics are taken from the questions in this PDF. Check the official syllabus and assignment paper for your session with IGNOU.

Assignment pattern

As printed on the first page of the PDF:

  • The cover prints: attempt both parts, maximum marks 100
  • Part A: five questions, about 500 words each, 5 x 10 = 50 marks
  • Part B: six activities are listed on the cover; evidence of any five is to be submitted, 5 x 10 = 50 marks

Always check your session's assignment paper and submission deadline on the official IGNOU website or with your study centre.
