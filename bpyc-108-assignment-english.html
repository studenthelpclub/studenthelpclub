Assignment pattern

As printed on the first page of the PDF:

  • The cover prints: answer all five questions, equal marks, Questions 1 and 2 in about 400 words each, attempt all parts of any question with parts
  • Questions 1 and 2 each print an OR alternative; the PDF solves the first option of each only
