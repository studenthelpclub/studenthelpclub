Topics covered in this PDF

BPAC-102 (Administrative Thinkers) - the questions solved in this PDF are on these topics:

  • Kautilya's principles of public administration, Chester Barnard on communication and authority, Maslow's theory of motivation, Peter Drucker, Likert's Interaction-Influence System
  • Argyris's Maturity-Immaturity Theory, Herbert Simon on choice and behaviour, Dwight Waldo and New Public Administration, Woodrow Wilson's era, elements of Policy Sciences

These topics are taken from the questions in this PDF. Check the official syllabus and assignment paper for your session with IGNOU.

Assignment pattern

As printed on the first page of the PDF:

  • The cover prints: Tutor Marked Assignment, total marks 100. Assignment A: two questions in about 500 words (20 each). Assignment B: four questions (Q3 to Q6) in about 250 words (10 each). Assignment C: four questions (Q7 to Q10) in about 100 words (6 each)
